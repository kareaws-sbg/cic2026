About the Event

Where Innovation Meets the Cloud

Cloud Innovators Challenge 2026 is a two-round cloud innovation hackathon designed to encourage students to solve real-world problems using AWS Cloud, Artificial Intelligence, and emerging technologies.

Participants will attend a comprehensive workshop followed by an intense hackathon where they build innovative cloud-based solutions, present their prototypes, and compete for exciting prizes.
